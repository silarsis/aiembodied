Face seam refinement

The same five characters, references and original 3D meshes, rebuilt with connected head boundaries and a matched appearance at the join. These are the exported VRMs in the app’s three-vrm renderer, with identical framing before and after.

The join is fixed to the same head bone on both sides and stays connected during expressions. The original overlapping strip is replaced with a bridge to the actual cut edge. Skin tone and boundary texture are matched while the interior speech, blink and emotion controls remain available.

Still experimental: this improves the face boundary; single-view texture fitting, hair, ears and neck shading can still show artefacts, particularly from the side. Teeth and tongue remain future work. Existing saved avatars are not rewritten.
